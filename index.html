Delete your PlotBop account

You can request permanent deletion of your account and associated data by email, even if you’ve already uninstalled the app.

Request deletion by email

Email xinmeidev001@gmail.com with the subject “PlotBop account deletion request.” Send it from the email address associated with your account if possible, and include your PlotBop user ID if you know it. Support may ask you to verify ownership before processing your request.

What happens to your data

Account deletion is permanent. It removes your PlotBop profile and account information, watch history, saved dramas, and unused coins and bonuses. Access tied to that PlotBop account, including unlocked content and membership benefits, will end.

Some records, such as transaction records, may be kept as long as necessary to meet legal obligations or for security and fraud prevention.

Active subscriptions

Cancel any active subscription in the store where you bought it to stop future renewals. Account deletion does not cancel your store subscription. You can still email us to request deletion if you need help.

Delete your account in the app

While signed in, open Me → Setting → About → Delete account. Read the deletion notice, check the acknowledgment, and confirm.
